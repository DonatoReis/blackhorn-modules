# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, technical, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, evidence, and outcomes.
- Respect privacy, authorization boundaries, and responsible disclosure.
- Do not publish credentials, private target data, or exploit details from
  systems you are not authorized to assess.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported through GitHub private vulnerability
reporting or directly to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit, reject, or
remove contributions and participation that violate this policy.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
